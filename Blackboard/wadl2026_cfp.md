# Web Archiving and Digital Libraries Workshop (WADL 2026)

- Full-day hybrid workshop, Friday 2026-10-16, Dallas TX, with JCDL 2026.
- **Submission deadline: 2026-09-16.** Notification 2026-10-01.
- Portal: https://easychair.org/conferences/?conf=wadl2026
- Organizers: Mat Kelly (Drexel), Brenda Reyes Ayala (Alberta).

## Submission types (from the full CFP page)

| type | length | for |
|---|---|---|
| short paper | 2+ pages | completed research, case studies, system descriptions, evaluations, lessons from practice |
| position paper | 2+ pages | perspectives, provocations, emerging challenges, future research proposals |
| poster | <= 2 pages | early-stage work, works in progress |
| demonstration | <= 2 pages | tools, systems, workflows |

No upper page limit is stated for short and position papers. Format: ACM
sigconf two-column (acmart, `sigconf` option); "another clear, professional
format will still be considered." Review is by program committee on relevance,
clarity, significance, technical soundness, discussion potential; nothing on
the page says double-blind, so the submission is prepared with author names.
At least one author registers and presents (in person or online). Accepted
work is listed on the site with optional open PDF; organizers plan deposit in
an open institutional repository; not a bar to extended archival publication.
Selected contributions may be invited to an extended post-workshop venue.

## Topical fit

Directly on the list: "archival standards, protocols, systems, and tools";
"AI and automation for archiving, description, discovery, and retrieval";
"metadata, classification, and machine-friendly discovery"; "interoperability
among web archiving systems"; "ethical, legal, and social questions" (the
publication-posture clause of draft -01).

## Decision taken 2026-09-07

Submit as a **short paper** (system description + lessons from practice), with
the Lear objections carried as a discussion section rather than filing it as a
position paper: the spec, the I-D, the two reference cards, and the
differential test are completed work. Target length 5-6 pages including
references.
